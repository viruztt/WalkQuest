Connecting your treadmill

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android phone (Web Bluetooth). Safari and Firefox don't support it.
  2. Close the treadmill's own phone app first. Most treadmills accept only one Bluetooth connection.
  3. Switch the treadmill on, then press Connect treadmill and choose it from the list.
  4. If it doesn't appear, try Show all Bluetooth devices. If it connects but no speed shows, open the Device log and share it.
